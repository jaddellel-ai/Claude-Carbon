N

Notes to the catalogue

What people ask before they start.

Note 1Is it really free?

Yes. Every programme is free and open, with no sign-up. Sign in with Google only if you want to save progress across devices.

Note 2Do I need to buy a book?

No. Books are optional field manuals. Every programme and certificate can be completed without them.

Note 3Is the certificate accredited?

Certificates show what you completed and how you were assessed. They are not issued by an accreditation body. Project managers can claim self-reported PDUs.

Note 4Are the companies real?

No. Halden Pumpworks, Veltara Systems and every case are fictional, built from real-world situations. Emission factors in simulations are rounded training values.
