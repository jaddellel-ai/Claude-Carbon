Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. Every programme is open and free during launch, with no sign-up. Sign in with Google only if you want to save progress across devices.

Will it stay free?

Foundations lessons, the live Season and Carbon Desk Case 01 stay free. Deeper programmes and certificates may become paid later. Anything you start for free stays free for you.

Do I need to buy a book?

No. Books are optional field manuals. Every programme and certificate can be completed without them.

Is the certificate accredited?

Certificates show what you completed and how you were assessed. They are not issued by an accreditation body. Project managers can claim self-reported PDUs.

Are the companies real?

No. Halden Pumpworks, Veltara Systems and every case are fictional, built from real-world situations. Emission factors in simulations are rounded training values.
